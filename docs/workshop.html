Electrical logic · schematic view

Functional nets; independent of wire color or physical jumper route
ECS selects e-ink; SDCS selects the Feather Friend microSD. SRCS is held high to keep the unused SRAM inactive. SCK/MOSI/MISO are shared SPI bus signals. Ground must be common.

Stage 03 / Circuit rules

Why these wires exist.

SPI allows several peripherals to share clock and data, with a separate chip-select per peripheral. This project proposes using the Feather Friend microSD for audio storage and the same Friend for screen updates.

  • Never wire the Feather Friend to 5V in this plan; use XIAO's regulated 3.3V.
  • MISO is needed for the microSD even though e-ink itself is generally write-only.
  • RST/BUSY: Feather Friend supplies display auto-reset; BUSY is optional for a delayed-refresh implementation.
  • Library support: your quad-color panel is JD79661; confirm the specific display driver and chip-select configuration in firmware.
The Sense expansion may also expose an SD interface that shares SPI lines. Configure or leave its SD chip select inactive so that only the intended Feather Friend microSD is selected. Confirm the expansion revision before flashing firmware.

Header soldering: where the iron actually goes

Solder header-to-board plated pads only. Never solder jumper cables into the breadboard.
The solder goes onto each board's metal pad where a header pin passes through—not the ESP32 chip, camera, e-ink ribbon, or removable jumper wires.

Stage 04 / Tools & technique

Minimal soldering, reliable connections.

  • Start with a fine chisel tip, flux and electronics solder. Use a temperature-controlled iron.
  • As a starting point, use around 320–350°C for leaded solder, or 350–380°C for many lead-free alloys; follow your solder's recommendations.
  • Place both header strips in the breadboard first. Set the XIAO on top, tack one pad at each end, verify alignment, then solder remaining pads.
  • Touch both the plated pad and header pin with the tip, feed a small amount of solder to the joint, then remove iron and hold still.
  • A good joint is smooth and joins pad to pin. Reflow dull, cracked or unsupported joints with flux. Remove bridges with solder wick.
  • Keep the LiPo battery, ribbon cable and microphone/camera assembly away from the hot iron.
Do not solder directly to the ESP32 package or other surface-mounted chips. You are attaching connectors to prepared through-hole/castellated pads only. If headers are already solidly soldered, skip the soldering step entirely.
